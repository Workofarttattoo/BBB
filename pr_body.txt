💡 What
Replaced multiple sequential generator expressions and list comprehensions with single O(N) loops in `get_business_metrics` and `generate_summary_report` across `qulab_rental_business.py`, `magic_rd_lab.py`, and `bbb_unified_business_library.py`.

🎯 Why
Dashboard rendering and metrics gathering functions were performing redundant iterations (up to 4-5 passes) over the same collections (`self.customers`, `self.sessions`, `businesses`). This caused unnecessary memory allocations and degraded performance as the datasets grew.

📊 Impact
Reduces time complexity of these aggregation methods from O(N * M) (where M is the number of distinct metrics being gathered) to a strict O(N). Prevents potential UI lag when loading business summaries for large libraries or rendering active dashboards for the magic lab and qulab businesses.

🔬 Measurement
Review the time taken to execute `get_business_metrics()` or `generate_summary_report()` on large datasets. They will perform linearly regardless of the number of unique sub-metrics calculated.
