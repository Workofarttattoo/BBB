💡 What: Consolidated multiple sequential list comprehensions and generator expressions into single O(N) `for` loops within `get_business_metrics` (`magic_rd_lab.py`, `qulab_rental_business.py`) and `generate_summary_report` (`bbb_unified_business_library.py`).
🎯 Why: To reduce redundant O(N) passes over collections and prevent unnecessary intermediate memory allocations during metric gathering, preventing event loop blocking.
📊 Impact: Reduces memory allocations and iterations from O(N * M) to O(N), improving performance for large collections.
