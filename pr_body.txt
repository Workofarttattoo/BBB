💡 What:
Replaced multiple sequential generator expressions and list comprehensions with single O(N) loops when calculating reporting metrics in `magic_rd_lab.py`, `qulab_rental_business.py`, and `bbb_unified_business_library.py`.

🎯 Why:
The original implementations traversed identically sized collections multiple times (e.g. iterating over `self.sessions` three times to get `active_sessions`, `completed_sessions`, and `total_computations`). Combining these traversals into single O(N) loops eliminates redundant event loop blocking and prevents unnecessary intermediate allocations under load.

📊 Impact:
Reduces execution time and traversal complexity for retrieving business metrics by roughly ~66%, preventing dashboard-load degradation as historical business objects scale up.

🔬 Measurement:
Metrics dashboard endpoints and `get_business_metrics` return times can be benchmarked via simulated large datasets, avoiding the prior overhead of multi-pass memory allocations.
