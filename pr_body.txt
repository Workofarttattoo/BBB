💡 What: Added `in_progress_tasks` tracking Set to `AutonomousBusinessOrchestrator` to avoid O(N) traversals of the unbounded `task_queue` whenever tasks in `IN_PROGRESS` status need to be accessed. `AutonomousTask` was made hashable to support this.
🎯 Why: Iterating over the entire, unbounded `task_queue` just to find a small subset of `IN_PROGRESS` tasks causes performance degradation as the task queue grows over the lifetime of the business.
📊 Impact: Reduced the time complexity of resolving orphaned tasks and querying in-progress tasks from O(N) to O(K) where K is the number of currently active tasks (which is strictly bounded by active agent counts and very small compared to total N tasks). Eliminates large event loop blocking during execution cycles.
🔬 Measurement: Verify tests run smoothly. Profiling `_execute_tasks_parallel` and `_reconcile_orphaned_in_progress_tasks` under load with thousands of queued tasks should show near-zero overhead.
