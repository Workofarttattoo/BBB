# QuLab Infinite — Company Identity & Execution Manual

## Founder Vision

QuLab Infinite was built from first principles by an independent researcher who needed better tools for rapid, honest iteration across quantum mechanics, materials science, and biology. The goal is **not to replace wet labs**, but to make them dramatically more efficient by providing **trustworthy computational co-pilots**.

The platform continues to evolve with:
- Transparent validation
- Rigorous testing against literature
- Commitment to clarity over hype

## Services & Pricing

### Discovery Hour — $100 USD

**One focused question per session:**
- Stress test one material
- Plan one synthesis route
- Run one FMO spectroscopy scenario

**Deliverables:**
- Live MCP (Model-Context-Protocol) access demo
- Concrete insights on your specific material/question
- Clear recommendation for next step (Pilot engagement)

**Rules:**
- Limit to one focused question per session
- Always end with clear recommendation for Pilot
- Zero-risk way to see gauntlet and report quality

**Positioning:** Low-commitment entry point — many consider $100 well spent to avoid $20k+ failed experiment.

---

### Pilot Engagement — Custom Quoted

**Full validation gauntlet on top candidate materials or synthesis routes.**

**Deliverables:**
- Comprehensive multi-physics simulation
- Literature validation report
- Confidence scores and limitation analysis
- Actionable recommendations for wet lab work

**Ideal For:** Teams who found value in Discovery Hour and want to de-risk their top candidates.

## Selling Philosophy (Cold Calling 3.0)

### Approach: Connection Not Interruption

**Principles:**
1. Warm the path with compelling messages before calling
2. Filter leads so every connection is qualified
3. Present as intelligent opportunity addressing buyer needs
4. Call with authority and make intelligent statements
5. Evolve pitch based on prospect responses
6. Invest in knowledge and presentation skills

### Mindset
- Decide success is non-negotiable (want it like air to breathe)
- Focus on abundance and opportunities others miss
- Action is everything — nothing happens until something is sold
- Prepare completely for every call
- Respect prospects and yourself always

### Avoid
- Numbers game / spray-and-pray cold calling
- Hard pitches like "what would it take to sign today"
- Treating selling as a dirty word
- Activity without strategy
