## Cold Call / Email Scripts

### Opener (Always Use This Structure)

> "Hi [Name], this is Joshua from QuLab Infinite. We help materials and quantum-biology teams de-risk ideas fast using multi-physics digital twins and validation gauntlets. We offer a low-commitment $100 one-hour Discovery Session where we run one of your materials live and give you concrete insights. Would you be open to booking a quick slot next week?"

### Personalization Hooks (Choose Based on Research)

**For Materials Science Teams:**
> "I saw your recent work on [specific material class]. Many teams in this space struggle with [specific challenge: e.g., 'predicting stability under operational conditions'] before committing to expensive synthesis. Our validation gauntlets can stress-test candidates computationally first."

**For Quantum Biology Labs:**
> "Your research on [specific system: e.g., 'photosynthetic energy transfer'] involves complex multi-physics interactions. We've helped similar labs identify promising optimization directions before wet lab iteration, reducing failed experiment costs."

**For Independent Researchers:**
> "As an independent researcher, you need rapid iteration without the overhead of large institutional resources. Our Discovery Hour gives you live MCP access to run one focused question with concrete insights for just $100."

### Value Framing (When Discussing Price)

> "A single failed lab experiment can easily cost $20k–$100k+. Our Discovery Hour often helps teams avoid one bad direction or identify a promising optimization — many consider that $100 well spent."

### Close (Always Use This)

> "Great — let's start with the $100 Discovery Hour so you can see the output quality firsthand. If it's useful, we can move to a full pilot on your top candidates."

## Objection Handling

### Price Pushback

**Prospect:** "$100 seems steep for an hour."

**Response:**
> "Most teams start with our $100 Discovery Hour so they can see the quality of the gauntlet and reports with zero risk. If it delivers value, we can discuss a full pilot. Think of it against the cost of even one failed experiment — $20k to $100k+. The Discovery Hour often helps avoid one bad direction."

### "We Already Have Computational Tools"

**Prospect:** "We use [DFT software/other tool] already."

**Response:**
> "That's great — many teams do. Our difference is multi-physics validation gauntlets tested against literature, not single-domain simulations. Plus, we position ourselves as co-pilots, not replacements. The Discovery Hour lets you compare output quality directly on your material. Zero risk."

### "Send Me Information First"

**Prospect:** "Can you send me a deck or case studies?"

**Response:**
> "Absolutely — I can share our methodology overview. But honestly, the best way to see the value is to run your own material live. That's why we offer the $100 Discovery Hour. You get concrete insights on your actual question, not generic examples. Would you be open to trying that first?"

## Compliance Rules (MUST FOLLOW)

### Tone Requirements
- ✅ Rigorous, transparent, humble, evidence-based
- ❌ NO hype language ("revolutionary", "breakthrough" without evidence)
- ❌ NO overpromising results
- ❌ NO black box claims
- ❌ NEVER say "replace your wet lab"

### Scientific Integrity
- Always validate claims against literature
- State clear confidence scores
- Acknowledge limitations explicitly
- Position as co-pilot, not replacement

### Pricing Communications
- Always frame $100 against $20k–$100k+ failed experiment cost
- Never pressure close
- Always offer zero-risk entry (Discovery Hour first)

## Revision Loop Guidance

If judge returns `revise`:

1. **Check which field failed** (personalization, tone, CTA clarity)
2. **Specific fixes:**
   - Low personalization → Add specific paper title, method, or finding reference
   - Hype detected → Replace with evidence-based language, add limitation acknowledgment
   - Unclear CTA → Make explicit: "Book 1-hour slot next week" not "Let me know if interested"
3. **Max 3 revision attempts**, then escalate to CEO for review
