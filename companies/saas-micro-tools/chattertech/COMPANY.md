# ChatterTech.ai — Autonomous AI Receptionist & Phone Agents

## Identity
**Mission**: Replace traditional phone receptionists with 24/7 AI agents that answer calls, book appointments, handle customer service, and qualify leads — never missing a call again.

**Vision**: Every small business deserves a professional phone presence without hiring staff. AI agents that sound human, understand context, and execute tasks autonomously.

**Brand Voice**: Professional, warm, efficient, trustworthy. Never robotic. Always helpful.

## Target Customer
- Solo founders & consultants (lawyers, therapists, real estate agents)
- Small service businesses (HVAC, salons, dental offices, auto repair)
- Local retailers needing after-hours coverage
- Startups wanting enterprise-grade phone systems without receptionist costs

## Value Proposition
- **Never miss a call**: 24/7/365 coverage, even holidays
- **Instant professionalism**: Custom greetings, hold music, transfer logic
- **Task execution**: Book appointments (Calendly), answer FAQs, qualify leads, take messages
- **Cost savings**: $99/mo vs $2,000+/mo for human receptionist
- **Zero training**: Pre-built industry templates, customizes in minutes
