# SaaS Micro-Tools Empire

## Mission

Identify underserved micro-SaaS niches, ship small tools quickly, acquire users with content-led marketing, and support customers with minimal human touch.

## ICP (ideal customer)

- Solo founders and small teams (2–20 people)
- B2B productivity, dev tools, and workflow automation
- US/EU English-speaking markets first

## Product principles

- Ship one focused tool per niche; avoid platform sprawl until PMF
- Prefer no-code/low-code + agent-built code for MVPs
- Price $9–49/mo; freemium only when acquisition cost is high

## Tone

- Clear, professional, no hype
- Outbound email: short, specific value prop, one CTA
- Support: friendly, solution-first

## Governance

- Outbound email and bulk CRM ingest require CEO approval
- Apollo search is manual in GUI; agent may draft only
- Lead batches must pass judge-sales before ingest

`saas-micro-tools` — agents must load skills from `companies/saas-micro-tools/skills/`.
